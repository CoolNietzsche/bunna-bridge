# Image generation prompts — Beersheba (Bunna Bridge)

Prompts for ChatGPT image generation, Manus or a similar tool, covering the platform, the roadmap phases, what is built, and brand and marketing scenes.

## How to use these

1. **Paste the style block below in front of any prompt** so every image looks like one family.
2. **Keep the words inside images short.** Image generators often misspell text, especially Amharic. Where a prompt puts words on the image, they are kept to a few English labels. Add longer text afterwards in Canva, Figma or Keynote.
3. **Pick an aspect ratio for where the image will go:**

   | Use | Ratio |
   |---|---|
   | Slides | 16:9 |
   | Instagram and LinkedIn posts | 1:1 or 4:5 |
   | Phone screens | 9:16 |
   | Website hero | 21:9 |

4. **If text comes out wrong**, add: *"Render all text exactly as written, in a clean sans-serif; if unsure, leave the label blank."*
5. **Do not ask for real logos** (EU, ECTA, banks, telecom companies). Ask for neutral, generic icons instead.

## Style block (paste in front of every prompt)

> Style: clean, premium, Apple-inspired product design. Soft natural light, generous white space, subtle depth and soft shadows, rounded corners. Colour palette: deep forest green (#1B7A4E and #125C3A), warm coffee-cherry red (#B2532E), cream and linen backgrounds (#F5F5F7, #FAF7F2), soft charcoal text (#1D1D1F). Ethiopian coffee origin feel: highland landscapes, shade trees, red cherries, raised drying beds. No clutter, no stock-photo clichés, no fake brand logos. Respectful, realistic depiction of Ethiopian people.
