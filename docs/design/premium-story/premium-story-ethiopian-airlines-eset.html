The people behind it

We pick only the red cherries. My father said a coffee tree remembers how you treat it.

Tadesse farms 1.8 hectares on the slopes above Bensa, under the shade of the old forest trees that keep the soil cool and the cherries ripening slowly. At harvest the family picks by hand, a few rounds over each tree, and carries the cherries to the washing station before evening.

This coffee was traced from his plot and his neighbour's to the bag in your cup, and both families were paid for every delivery.
