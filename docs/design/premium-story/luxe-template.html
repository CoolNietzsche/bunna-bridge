Meet the farmers

Two families grew this coffee on the hills above Bensa. Their story is shared with their consent.

We pick only the red cherries. A coffee tree remembers how you treat it.

Tadesse farms 1.8 hectares under old forest shade trees. The family picks by hand, several rounds per tree, and carries the cherries to the washing station before evening.
